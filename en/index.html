About the house

A homely stay, steps from the sea

Bonita offers 3 individually furnished rooms with private bathrooms and terraces. The rooms are in a three-storey house with an attic floor, and the garden has two large gazebos and a canopy where you can relax among the greenery.

The house is a 2–3 minute walk from the South Beach and 10 minutes from the North Beach, at the entrance of the Sea Garden and the harbour. Shops, restaurants and entertainment are all close by.

  • Private bathroom and air conditioning in every room
  • Garden with two gazebos and a canopy
  • Parking and internet
